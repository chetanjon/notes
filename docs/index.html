Matte

A dark, minimal notes app for iPhone. Write, search, make checklists, and pin one note to your Lock Screen. On your Home Screen it is called Notes.

Pin a note to the Lock Screen

Long-press a note in the list and choose Pin to Lock Screen, or tap the pin in the editor. The note appears under the clock on your Lock Screen straight away, and in the Dynamic Island on phones that have one. The card shows the note's title, and for a checklist how many items are done, like "0/4". A line that ends in a number, like "Water 3", gets a + you can tap to count. Tap the card to open the note. Pin a different note and it takes the place of the old one.

iOS clears any app's Lock Screen card after eight hours. Open Notes and the pinned note comes back. If you would rather it never left, add the Pinned note widget once: press and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, choose Lock Screen, tap the area under the clock, and pick it. The round one is a ring of your checklist's progress; the wide one is the card. The round Notes widget beside it is a pencil: tap it for a new note.

Home Screen widget

Press and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and pick Notes. The small one is a pencil: tap it and you are writing a new note. The medium and large ones list your latest notes, the pinned one first; tap a note to open it, or the pencil for a new one.

Checklists

Tap the checklist button in the editor to turn the current line into an item. Tap the circle to tick it. Return continues the list; Return on an empty item ends it.

Writing Tools

On an iPhone with Apple Intelligence, select some text in a note and choose Writing Tools to proofread, rewrite, or summarise it, right in the note. It is Apple's feature and it works the same here as in Apple's own apps. Checklist circles stay where they are.

Siri and search

Your notes show up when you search your iPhone from the Home Screen; tap one to open it. Siri takes three things without any setup: say "New note in Matte", "Add to Groceries in Matte" (Siri asks what to add, and it goes on the list), or "Pin Groceries in Matte". Say the name of the note as it appears in the list. "In Notes" works as well.

Deleting

Swipe a note left to delete it. It goes to the Trash, behind the trash icon beside the title. Open the Trash and tap a note to put it back, or swipe it to delete it for good. Anything left in the Trash is deleted after 30 days.

Sync

Notes sync between your own devices through iCloud when you are signed in. There is no account to create. If notes are not appearing on a second device, check that both are signed into the same Apple Account and that iCloud Drive is on in Settings → your name → iCloud.

Something wrong?

Open an issue on the project's GitHub issue tracker with what you expected and what happened.
