Notes

A dark, minimal notes app for iPhone. Write, search, make checklists, and pin one note to your Lock Screen.

Pin a note to the Lock Screen

Long-press a note in the list and choose Pin to Lock Screen, or tap the pin in the editor. The note appears under the clock on your Lock Screen straight away, and in the Dynamic Island on phones that have one. A checklist shows its open items and how many are done. A line that ends in a number, like "Water 3", gets a + you can tap to count. A long list shows "+N more": tap it to see the rest, and tap the title to come back to the top. Tap the title to open the note. Pin a different note and it takes the place of the old one.

iOS clears any app's Lock Screen card after eight hours. Open Notes and the pinned note comes back. If you would rather it never left, add the Notes widget once: press and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, choose Lock Screen, tap the area under the clock, and add Notes.

Checklists

Tap the checklist button in the editor to turn the current line into an item. Tap the square to tick it. Return continues the list; Return on an empty item ends it.

Sync

Notes sync between your own devices through iCloud when you are signed in. There is no account to create. If notes are not appearing on a second device, check that both are signed into the same Apple Account and that iCloud Drive is on in Settings → your name → iCloud.

Something wrong?
